## 📌 About War Computer

War Computer is a computer and technology retail business serving customers in Pakistan, with a physical business presence in **Saddar, Karachi** and online product browsing for customers across Pakistan.

The website provides a modern digital storefront where customers can explore computer products, compare specifications, check prices and availability, and contact the business for product inquiries.

# 🖼️ Image SEO

Product images are an important part of a computer e-commerce website.

Images should use:

* Descriptive filenames
* Appropriate image dimensions
* Compressed formats where practical
* Descriptive `alt` text
* Relevant surrounding product text

Example:

```text
dell-latitude-7490-core-i5-laptop.jpg
```

instead of:

```text
IMG00123.jpg
```

Example alt text:

```html
alt="Dell Latitude 7490 Core i5 business laptop"
```

Google recommends placing high-quality images near relevant text and providing useful context so search engines and users can understand them.
