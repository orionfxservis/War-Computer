Cookie Policy

Executive Summary

War Computers uses essential first-party cookies and browser LocalStorage solely to maintain your active shopping cart, preserve your preferred color theme, and remember your wholesale vs. retail view. We never deploy invasive third-party ad tracking pixels or cross-site fingerprinting scripts.

1. What Are Cookies and LocalStorage?

Cookies and HTML5 LocalStorage are small data files stored locally on your computer or mobile device by your web browser. They allow our web store to remember your actions and preferences over a period of time, ensuring you do not have to re-enter them whenever you return to the site or navigate between pages.

2. Essential Technologies We Employ

🛒
Shopping Cart Session (war_computers_cart) Preserves chosen laptop configurations, quantities, and selected components across browser refreshes so your cart remains intact.
🎨
Color Theme Selection (war_computers_site_theme) Remembers your selected aesthetic theme (Theme 1 War Blue, Theme 2 Tech Cyan, Theme 3 Premium Gold, Theme 4 Modern Red, Theme 5 Clean White).
💼
Pricing Mode Preference (war_computers_pricing_mode) Maintains whether you are shopping in Retail Direct Mode or B2B Wholesale Tiered Discount Mode.

3. Zero Advertising or Behavioral Profiling

War Computers operates an ethical, hardware-first trading business. We do not sell user data to advertising exchanges, and we do not permit external behavioral remarketing networks to profile our visitors.

4. Managing & Clearing Browser Storage

You can block or delete cookies and LocalStorage via your browser preferences (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) at any time. Please note that clearing LocalStorage will reset your saved shopping cart and restore the default color theme.
