Executive Summary

War Computers operates as Pakistan’s premier wholesale and retail computer distributor in Saddar Karachi. We treat all client records, quotations, dispatch manifests, and B2B orders with strict confidentiality. We do not sell or trade your personal data to external marketing syndicates.

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential customer data including recipient name, phone/WhatsApp number, delivery address (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and nationwide), NTN/STRN for corporate tax invoices, and hardware quotation specifications needed for fulfillment.

2. Payment Security & Banking Privacy

War Computers never records or stores debit/credit card CVVs or banking PINs on its servers. Bank wire transfers, Raast instant payments, Easypaisa, JazzCash, and COD receipts are processed through secure encrypted channels.

3. Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Restrictions

We do not monetize or distribute client contact lists. Information shared with authorized courier networks (TCS, Leopards, Daewoo Cargo) is limited strictly to shipping waypoint data.
